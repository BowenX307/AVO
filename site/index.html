Privacy & safety

Your screen, your call.

01 · Data

Screenshots go to the Meta Model API. AVO calls Muse in Standard mode, where your content isn’t used to train Meta’s models.

02 · Safety checks

Before each step AVO rings what it’s about to act on and counts down a second — say “no” to call it off. Anything that can’t be undone (send, submit, delete, pay), and any other warning from Meta, waits for your “yes”.

03 · Control

Pause or stop any time. Touch the mouse or keyboard and AVO pauses; fling the pointer into a screen corner to stop it at once.
